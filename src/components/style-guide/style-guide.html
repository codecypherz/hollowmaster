Rarity frames

The frame's metal shifts across the star range so rarity is recognisable before the stars are read. Shown at one size, side by side, because the tiers are only judgeable against one another. Reinforcement only — the star track states rarity on its own.

Card at any size

The card fills the width it is given and derives its height from the fixed ratio; everything inside scales from the card's own width. The ladder starts at the card's minimum supported width of {{ minWidth }}px — nothing on this page renders one narrower — and straddles the {{ abilityGate }}px ability gate a pixel apart, so the section can be seen appearing without the surface telling the card anything. Ornament simplifies toward the minimum; sections, star slots, bars, labels, and chevrons never do.
